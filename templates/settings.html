About Sliding Window Detection

Unlike naive counters that reset at arbitrary intervals, the sliding window examines contiguous login events per IP address.

  • • Bursts: An attacker attempting 5 passwords in 10 seconds is flagged immediately.
  • • Slow Probing: Attempts spaced farther apart than the time window are recognized as lower risk.
  • • Session Persistence: Settings are stored securely in your active session.
